What is Interoperability? Interoperability in manufacturing is the ability of different industrial systems, equipment, and devices to connect, communicate, and exchange information seamlessly across production processes. It allows hardware and software to support each other, enabling partners, including customers, suppliers, and various departments, to share information quickly and accurately, regardless of their manufacturer or design.
