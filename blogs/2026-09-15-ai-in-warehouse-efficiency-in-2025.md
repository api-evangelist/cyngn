The History of Warehouse Operations The history of warehouse operations dates back to ancient civilizations, but the modern concept of warehousing began to take shape during the Industrial Revolution in the 18th and 19th centuries. As mass production and global trade increased, the need for effective warehouse management, efficient storage, and timely distribution of goods became paramount.
